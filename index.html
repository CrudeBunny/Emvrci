What can I test here?

Photo resizing and JPEG quality, PNG transparency, verified ZIP packaging, MP3 encoding, saving, Home Screen launch and offline reopening after an online visit.

This is a browser prototype of part of EmVRCompress 0.4.0, not the Windows executable or a native iOS build. HEVC output, HDR video, Epres, PDF optimization, RBDB creation/restoration and iTunes library import are not ported. Protected M4P is rejected. Keep using the desktop application for those operations.

Keep the app in the foreground during conversion. Safari may stop background processing or evict cached files.

Open from an HTTPS website for Home Screen and offline testing.
